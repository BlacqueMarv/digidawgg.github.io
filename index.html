Why it matters

The financial sector is ground zero for data breaches.

U.S. data breaches hit a record high in 2024 — 3,322 reported incidents. Financial services firms led all industries with 739 breaches. Most investment apps store your portfolio on their servers. That makes you a target every time they get hit.
