Frequently Asked Questions

Is my portfolio data safe?

Yes. All portfolio data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted to any server. Your data is protected by your device's built-in OS encryption — Android Keystore on Android 10+ and iOS Keychain on iOS 16+.

Does Stock-Dawgg require an account?

No. Stock-Dawgg requires no account, no login, and no email address. Install the app and start monitoring immediately.

How do price alerts work?

Stock-Dawgg monitors your watchlist in the background and sends a push notification — and triggers the Barking Dawgg animation — when a holding moves beyond your set threshold. You can configure separate thresholds for stocks and crypto.

Does crypto monitoring work 24/7?

Yes. Crypto is monitored around the clock since crypto markets never close. Stock alerts respect market hours, with an optional extended hours toggle for pre-market and after-hours activity.

What are the monthly PDF reports?

On the 1st of each month, Stock-Dawgg automatically generates a PDF summary of your portfolio performance. Reports are built entirely on your device and saved to Downloads/StockDawgg/. They are never uploaded or shared.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app. All locally stored data — watchlist, portfolio, preferences, and cached data — is permanently deleted upon uninstallation. PDF reports saved to Downloads/StockDawgg/ can be deleted manually from your file manager.

What devices are supported?

Stock-Dawgg supports Android 10 (API 29) and above, and iOS 16 and above. The app is available for both phone and tablet.
